Welcome to Rizal Park

Rizal Park, also known as Luneta Park, is one of the most famous historical parks in the Philippines. Located in Manila, it honors Dr. Jose Rizal and his contribution to Philippine independence.

Why Visit Rizal Park?

History

Learn about Philippine history and national heroes.

Nature

Enjoy beautiful gardens and relaxing outdoor spaces.

Activities

Explore attractions, take pictures, and spend time with family.
