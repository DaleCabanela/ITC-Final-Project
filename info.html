Visitor Information

Important information before visiting Rizal Park.

Location

Rizal Park is located in Ermita, Manila, Philippines.

Visitor Reminders

  • Keep the park clean.
  • Dispose of trash properly.
  • Respect historical monuments.
  • Follow park rules and regulations.
  • Take care of your personal belongings.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Rizal Park open to visitors?

Rizal Park is a public park. Check current park advisories before visiting.

Can visitors take pictures?

Visitors can generally take personal photographs in public areas.

Is there an entrance fee?

General park access is free, but some activities or facilities may have separate fees.
